Why Regular Upholstery Cleaning Matters

What looks clean can still be holding onto everyday residue.

Daily use can leave dust, body oils, perspiration, food residue, pet allergens and odours within the fibres — even when the surface still looks reasonably clean.

Dust & allergens

Dust, pollen, pet allergens and dust-mite allergen material can settle into sofas, mattresses and carpets.

Body oils & perspiration

Daily contact transfers oils and moisture into fabric, contributing to residue, marks and stale odours.

Food & drink residue

Spills and crumbs may leave stains, sticky residue and unwanted smells if they remain in the fabric.

Odours & trapped residue

Accumulated soil, moisture and residue can contribute to stale smells and make upholstery feel less fresh.

✓

Honest results. Cleaning can sometimes make an item look close to new. However, ageing, fading, permanent discolouration and previous fabric damage may remain.

The Process

How Skrub.Lab Does It.

A careful process designed to loosen, lift and extract dirt and residue from the fabric. The exact method is adjusted to the upholstery and its condition.

01

Inspect

Check the fabric, condition, stains, existing discolouration and any areas that need extra care.

02

Dry Vacuum

Remove loose dust, hair and debris before introducing moisture.

03

Pre-treat

Apply a suitable cleaning solution to target general soil and heavily used areas.

04

Agitate

Work the solution gently through the fabric where needed to help loosen residue.

05

Extract

Rinse and extract loosened dirt and residue from the upholstery.

06

Dry Pass & Finish

Additional extraction passes help reduce moisture, followed by a final inspection. Steam treatment may be used where suitable.
