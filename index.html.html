Why regular upholstery cleaning matters

What can collect in everyday fabric?

Even when upholstery looks clean, daily use can leave dust, body oils, perspiration, food residue, pet allergens and odours within the fibres.

Dust & allergens Dust, pollen, pet allergens and dust-mite allergen material can settle into sofas, mattresses and carpets.
Body oils & perspiration Daily contact transfers oils and moisture into fabric, contributing to residue, marks and stale odours.
Food & drink residue Spills and crumbs may leave stains, sticky residue and unwanted smells if they remain in the fabric.
Odours & trapped residue Accumulated soil, moisture and residue can contribute to stale smells and make upholstery feel less fresh.
Honest results: Cleaning can sometimes make an item look close to new. However, ageing, fading and permanent discolouration may remain.
Our Process

How we Skrub.

A careful process designed to loosen, lift and extract dirt and residue from the fabric.

01

Inspect

Check the fabric, stains, colour and overall condition.

02

UV-assisted Vacuum

Remove loose dust, hair and surface debris before wet cleaning.

03

Pre-treat

Apply a suitable cleaning solution to help loosen dirt and residue.

04

Agitate (Skrub! Time)

Work the solution through the fabric to loosen embedded soil.

05

Extract

Lift out dirty solution, loosened dirt and excess moisture.

06

Dry Passes

Perform additional extraction passes to reduce remaining moisture.

07 · OPTIONAL

Steam Sanitisation

Additional heat-based treatment for suitable fabrics.
